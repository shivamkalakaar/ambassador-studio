Why a mascot, not a person

We made human ads first. Emily looks real and speaks naturally. Side by side, the mascot felt warmer, more on-brand and more fun to watch, so we chose it for Coupa.

  • Characters build memoryBrand-owned characters did almost twice as well as celebrity ads at growing market share in System1’s research.
  • No one mistakes it for a real personRealistic AI people can lose viewers’ trust, especially in video. A mascot is clearly a character.
  • It never leavesA mascot never ages, never has a scandal and works in any language.
