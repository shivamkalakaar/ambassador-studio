Meet Cocoa, Coupa Café’s mascot.

We design a character that belongs to one brand. Then we make its video ads every month and run its social account. These are Cocoa’s first two ads, so turn your sound on.

The version we didn’t pick

First we tried a person.

Emily is an AI-generated woman we made from scratch and placed at the Coupa counter. She looks real and talks naturally. Next to Cocoa, though, she felt less warm and less like Coupa, so we went with the mascot.

People remember charactersIn System1’s research, brand-owned characters grew market share almost twice as much as celebrity ads.
Nobody thinks Cocoa is realViewers trust realistic AI people less, especially in video. A felt squirrel doesn’t have that problem.
It won’t age out of the jobCocoa won’t get older or turn up in a tabloid. And it can do the same ad in Spanish.

Socials

Ground rules

What we won’t do

  • Pass AI off as real.Every ad and every post says it’s AI-generated.
  • Copy anyone.No lookalikes of real people or of another brand’s character.
  • Fake a review.The character presents the product. It never says it tried it.
  • Reuse your character.It works for you and nobody else.
